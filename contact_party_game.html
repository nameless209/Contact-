How to play Contact? 💡

1. Spin the Wheel: Select or create a player group and click the wheel to choose the Word Keeper.

2. Set Secret Word: The Word Keeper privately types a word or chooses one from the Easy, Medium, or Hard Word Bank.

3. Real-Life Room Play: The screen shows only the first letter followed by dashes (e.g., B _ _ _ _).

4. Shout "CONTACT!": Guessers give clues to each other for words starting with the revealed letters. When two guessers agree on a word before the Keeper guesses it, shout "CONTACT!" and count 3-2-1! If they say the same word, the Keeper must tap Reveal Next Letter 🔤.
